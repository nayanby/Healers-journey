## Suggested tester invitation

Try Life RPG for two weeks. Add activities in Learning, Work, Fitness, Social and Creativity. Complete a few, undo one, check the daily badge shelf and graph, and try changing an outfit or companion. Please report any crashes, confusing screens, missing rewards or incorrect progress, with your phone model and Android version.

## Test checklist

1. First launch and character selection.
2. Add a saved activity and a new task to Today.
3. Complete, undo and remove tasks; check XP and coins each time.
4. Earn the three-category and five-category coin bonuses on separate days.
5. Check badge shelf, graph periods, achievements and journal.
6. Add and complete a care routine; check character appearance.
7. Buy and equip an outfit and companion.
8. Relaunch and confirm progress persists.

Google Play may require at least 12 testers opted in continuously for 14 days before a newer personal developer account can request production access. Closed-test setup itself also needs a tester email list or Google Group, available countries, and the Play Console policy declarations.
