## Full description

Make your real life the adventure.

Life RPG turns everyday activities into quests for your own character. Choose what matters today, complete a task, and watch your progress take shape through XP, coins, badges and achievements.

BUILD YOUR DAY
Choose saved activities from Learning, Work, Fitness, Social and Creativity, or create a new task. Your Today list keeps completed tasks visible so you can see what you did.

GROW ACROSS FIVE AREAS
Each completed activity adds to your character's progress. See how the five areas change over a day, week, month, year or your whole journey.

COLLECT YOUR PROGRESS
Your daily badge shelf shows which areas you explored. Finish activities in three different areas for a coin bonus, and reach all five for another bonus. Milestones and achievements give you more to work toward.

MAKE THE HERO YOURS
Earn coins through activities, then use them to unlock outfits and companions. Choose a character and build a look that feels like you.

CARE FOR YOUR CHARACTER
Keep simple care routines and see your character's appearance respond to how you care for them.

Start with one small step. Let your journey grow from there.
