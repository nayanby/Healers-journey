# Colorful Life RPG icons

Original colorful transparent PNG illustrations generated with the built-in image_gen tool. Rounded 3D clay forms share cobalt blue, teal, coral, ivory, and gold. Full-resolution masters are stored in colorful/. Android receives 192px transparent exports after all 46 masters are complete.

The app must preserve the artwork colors. Do not apply monochrome tinting to these PNGs. Keep the same asset for a meaning in navigation, skill cards, and actions. Icons sit directly on the theme surface, with no baked-in tile.

Prompt set: color-prompts.json. Export/integration script: ../../tools/Integrate-ColorIcons.ps1.
