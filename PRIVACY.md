# Life RPG Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 October 2026

Life RPG (`com.life.rpg`) lets you plan activities, record care routines, and track game progress. Your activity entries, character choices, achievements, and settings are stored on your device.

This release does not require an account and does not contain advertising or analytics. It does not send your activity data to a Life RPG server. Google Drive sync is unavailable in this release because it has not been configured.

If you choose to export your data, the destination you select controls that copy. Android's device backup or transfer features may also copy app data according to your device and Google account settings. Those services are governed by their own privacy terms.

You can delete data stored on your device by clearing Life RPG's storage or uninstalling the app. Copies you exported or backed up may need to be deleted separately from their destination.

We will update this policy before enabling any new online data features.

For privacy questions, contact **nayanby@gmail.com**.
