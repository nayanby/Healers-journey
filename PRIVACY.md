# Life RPG Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 October 2026

Life RPG (`com.life.rpg`) lets you plan activities, record care routines, and track game progress. Your activity entries, journal thoughts, character choices, achievements, and settings are stored on your device.

This release does not require an account and does not contain advertising. It does not send your activity data to a Life RPG server. Google Drive sync is unavailable in this release because it has not been configured.

Life RPG uses Google Play Games Services for player sign-in. Google Play services may reconnect an existing Play Games profile using your device account settings. You can also choose to sign in from Life RPG's Settings. When connected, Life RPG reads your player display name to show the connection status. This integration does not send your activity entries, care routines, or financial records to Play Games. The Play Games SDK automatically collects analytics and diagnostics for SDK stability and product improvements, and Google processes Play Games profile and service information under its own [Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). Play Games account management and deletion of Google-held game data are available through your [Play Games profile](https://play.google.com/games/profile).

If you choose to export your data, the destination you select controls that copy. Android's device backup or transfer features may also copy app data according to your device and Google account settings. Those services are governed by their own privacy terms.

## Deleting your data

To erase Life RPG's local activities, journal thoughts, care records, financial records, milestones, character choices, and progress, open Journal → Settings → Danger zone → Reset all game data. You can also clear the app's storage or uninstall it. Life RPG does not keep a server copy of these records or impose a separate retention period. Delete any exported or backed-up copies from the locations where you saved them.

To remove Life RPG data held by Google Play Games, visit your [Play Games profile](https://play.google.com/games/profile), open Your Data, and choose Delete Play Games Account & Data. Find Life RPG in the individual-game section and select its deletion option. Google's [deletion guide](https://support.google.com/googleplay/answer/9130646) explains this process and the separate controls for Google Web & App Activity. Google controls retention of its service diagnostics under its own privacy policies; Life RPG cannot delete Google's diagnostic records directly.

For help finding the right deletion controls, email **nayanby@gmail.com**. We do not require you to create a separate Life RPG online account to request help.

We will update this policy before enabling any new online data features.
